The two tracks

Affiliate, or your own brand.

Same engine underneath. The difference is whose name is on the front and how deep the economics go.

AF-01 / AFFILIATE

Affiliate

Refer business owners with your tracking link. We close, fund, and fulfill. You earn on qualified outcomes.

Two tiers
Commission schedule provided at approval, in your partner agreement
Your referrals are attributed at first touch and stay yours — attribution is sticky and never overwritten.
Commissions accrue on qualified, completed client outcomes: funded engagements and completed repair enrollments.
Tier 2 unlocks when your first referral funds, or when you recruit your first affiliate — and adds an override on your downline's qualified outcomes.
Referral dashboard: your links, your pipeline, your balance, your payout status.
Open application. Approval required before your link goes live.
Apply as an affiliate
WL-01 / WHITE-LABEL

White-Label Partner

Your brand on the front. Our engine, team, and rails behind it. Built for funders, brokers, and audiences that deserve their own name.

50% revenue share
Invite and approval only
Your clients see your brand. Your dedicated partner portal shows your clients — and only your clients.
Zero fulfillment on your side. Underwriting, funding operations, inquiry removal, document handling, and compliance rails all run on fundhub.
50% share of revenue on your book, per the partner agreement.
The same UnderwriteIQ engine, deliverables, and advisor workflow that runs fundhub.ai.
Application, review, and a signed partner agreement before launch.
