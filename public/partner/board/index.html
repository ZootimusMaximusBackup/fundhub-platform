The Pain
A Swipe File Tells You What Someone Ran. Not What Survived.

Anyone can screenshot an ad. That tells you it existed. It doesn't tell you whether it made money — and a losing ad and a winning ad look identical in a screenshot.

So people copy the wrong things. They copy an ad that ran for four days and got killed, and they wonder why their version doesn't work either.

Longevity is the only public signal that's hard to fake. Nobody keeps paying to run an ad that loses money. An ad still running after ninety days is telling you something a screenshot never will.
