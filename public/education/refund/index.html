Refund Policy

Effective date: July 15, 2026  ·  FUNDHUB LLC · Fundhub Education

1. The Short Version

You may request a full refund within 3 days of purchase, provided you have not completed more than the first module or downloaded the template library. After that window, tuition is non-refundable except as stated below or required by law.

2. Why This Policy

These are digital programs with immediate full access. Once substantial content is consumed or templates are downloaded, the product has been delivered.

3. How to Request

Email support@fundhubeducation.com from your enrollment email with the subject "Refund Request" and your order details. Eligible refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days.

4. Financed Tuition

If tuition was financed at checkout, an approved refund is applied against the financing balance per the checkout provider's process; their terms govern the mechanics of the reversal.

5. Duplicate or Erroneous Charges

Duplicate or erroneous charges are corrected promptly. Contact us with the transaction details and we will correct it promptly.

6. Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is incorrect, please contact us first. We resolve legitimate billing issues quickly, and a direct request is almost always faster than a card dispute.

7. Statutory Rights

Nothing in this policy limits any non-waivable refund or cancellation rights you may have under the laws of your state or country. Where such laws provide greater rights, those laws control.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@fundhubeducation.com.
