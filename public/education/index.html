Program CM / Consumer credit

Credit Mastery System

Most people never learn how the credit reporting system works, so they never learn how to manage their own file. This program teaches the full picture: the reporting system, the scoring models, your rights under federal law, and the exact process for identifying and disputing inaccurate information on your own reports, in your own name, by your own hand.

Built for: individuals who want to understand, correct, and manage their own consumer credit file without hiring anyone to do it for them.

Curriculum10 modules · 40+ video lessons
CM-01
How Consumer Credit Reporting Works
The three bureaus, data furnishers, and how information moves through the reporting system.
CM-02
Reading Your Three Reports
Pulling all three reports and decoding every section, field, and code they contain.
CM-03
How Scores Are Calculated
FICO and VantageScore factors, weightings, and what actually moves a score over time.
CM-04
Identifying Errors and Inaccuracies
Auditing your own file for inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or unverifiable information.
CM-05
Your Rights Under Federal Law
The Fair Credit Reporting Act in plain English: what you are entitled to and how to use it.
CM-06
Drafting Your Bureau Disputes
Writing accurate, specific dispute letters using the included template library.
CM-07
Disputing With Creditors Directly
When and how to write to the companies furnishing the data, not just the bureaus.
CM-08
Tracking, Follow-Up, and Escalation
Documentation systems, response timelines, and what to do at each stage of the process.
CM-09
Credit Optimization
Utilization strategy, account aging, credit mix, and limit management for a healthy file.
CM-10
Rebuilding and Long-Term Maintenance
Establishing positive history, monitoring your file, and keeping it clean for good.

What you will be able to do

  • Read any consumer credit report line by line and understand every entry on it.
  • Audit your own file and identify inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable information.
  • Draft, send, and track your own dispute letters to bureaus and creditors.
  • Manage follow-up, documentation, and escalation through the full process.
  • Build and maintain a long-term plan for a strong, accurate credit file.

Program CS / Business credit & lending

Capital Strategy Program

Lending decisions are not mysterious. They follow criteria, and those criteria can be learned. This program teaches how business credit profiles are actually built, how lenders evaluate businesses and their owners, and how to prepare your company, your documents, and your applications before you ever approach a lender on your own.

Built for: business owners and entrepreneurs who want to understand business credit and lending well enough to handle it themselves.

Curriculum10 modules · 40+ video lessons
CS-01
Entity Foundations
Business structure, EIN, licensing, and the identity elements lenders verify first.
CS-02
The Business Credit Bureaus
Dun & Bradstreet, Experian Commercial, and Equifax Business: what each tracks and reports.
CS-03
Building Vendor Credit
Establishing a business credit profile step by step through tiered vendor accounts.
CS-04
Personal Credit in Business Lending
Where the owner's file matters, where it does not, and how the two interact.
CS-05
Banking and Financial Statements
The accounts, statements, and financial records lenders actually read, and how they read them.
CS-06
How Lenders Underwrite
Criteria, ratios, and red flags across different lender types, explained from the inside.
CS-07
The Capital Landscape
Business credit cards, lines of credit, term loans, and SBA programs: how each product works and who each is designed for.
CS-08
Application Strategy
Sequencing, documentation, and presentation: preparing a complete, accurate application package.
CS-09
Relationship Banking
Working with bankers over time and why the relationship changes what is possible.
CS-10
Capital Planning
Matching capital types to business stages and building a long-term capital strategy.

What you will be able to do

  • Understand exactly what lenders evaluate before they ever see an application.
  • Build a business credit profile from zero using a structured, tiered approach.
  • Read your own financials the way an underwriter reads them.
  • Prepare complete, accurate application packages on your own.
  • Evaluate capital products and offers and match them to your business stage.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is this a loan or a funding service?
No. Fundhub Education is an education company. We do not lend money, broker loans, or arrange financing of any kind. Our programs teach you how credit and lending work so you can act on your own. If you are looking for funding, that is fundhub.ai.
Do you repair my credit for me?
No. We do not perform credit repair services and we never contact credit bureaus or creditors on your behalf. The Credit Mastery System teaches you the process and gives you the templates. Every letter is drafted, signed, and sent by you.
Do you guarantee results?
No. We make no guarantees about credit scores, dispute outcomes, lending approvals, or financial results of any kind. Outcomes depend on your specific situation and your own effort. What we guarantee is the quality and completeness of the curriculum itself.
How is the program delivered?
Entirely online and self-paced. After enrollment you receive login credentials to the student portal, where all video lessons, templates, and workbooks are available immediately. You keep lifetime access, including future updates.
How long does it take to complete?
Each program contains 10 modules with 40+ video lessons. Most students complete a program in 4 to 8 weeks working a few hours per week, but there is no deadline. The material is yours for life.
What is the refund policy?
You may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase, provided you have accessed less than 20% of the program content. Full details are in our Refund Policy.
Can I pay in installments?
Flexible payment options are shown at checkout where available. Any financing offered at checkout is provided by an independent third-party payment provider, subject to its own terms and approval process. Fundhub Education is not the lender.
