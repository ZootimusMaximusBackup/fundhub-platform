Process

How it works

A defined path from application to funding. No obligation to proceed at any step.

01 / APPLY

Submit your details

Complete a short application with your business information. The initial review is a soft inquiry and does not affect your credit score.

02 / REVIEW

Review your options

A funding advisor reviews your profile and outlines the financing options available to your business.

03 / FUND

Move forward

Select an option, complete the required documentation, and funds are disbursed by the funding partner.

The engine

Underwriting, run by software.

Fundhub is built around UnderwriteIQ, an underwriting engine that reads your profile the way funding partners do, before you commit to anything.

UnderwriteIQ · Operational
E-01 / INTAKE

One submission

You provide your details once. The engine assembles your profile from the information you submit and a soft inquiry.

E-02 / ANALYSIS

Structured analysis

UnderwriteIQ maps your profile against funding partner criteria and structures the options worth a closer look.

E-03 / REVIEW

Human review

A funding advisor walks the output with you. Software does the reading. A person goes through the deciding with you.

Financing we help you access

Coordinated 0% intro APR business credit lines opened across multiple issuers.

Guarantees

Three guarantees. In writing.

You're protected three ways. If we don't hold up our end, you don't pay.

G-01 / 24 HOURS

24-Hour Action Guarantee

Once you're signed on, a funding advisor begins working your file within 24 business hours. If we don't act in that window, you're protected and you don't pay.

G-02 / 72 HOURS

72-Hour Application Guarantee

Once you're signed on, your applications go out within 72 business hours. If we don't act in that window, you're protected and you don't pay.

G-03 / FUNDING

Funding Guarantee

If you qualify and we don't secure funding for you, you don't pay. If you get nothing, we earn nothing.

Protection terms for each engagement type are set out in the service agreement; where an engagement includes an upfront deposit, these guarantees provide for its return under the stated conditions. These guarantees are not a guarantee of credit approval, funding amounts, rates, or terms, which are determined by funding partners.
