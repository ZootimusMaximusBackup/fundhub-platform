alerts

Alerts raised against clients, and the trigger rules that raise them.

Nothing on this page is sent to a client. An alert is a note in this queue for whoever is looking at it. There is no email, no text message and no other outbound path behind any of it. The wording of an alert restates figures that are already on file — a percentage, a threshold, a score — and says nothing about whether anybody's credit is good or bad. Every rule starts switched off with no threshold, and it stays that way until somebody chooses a number here.
