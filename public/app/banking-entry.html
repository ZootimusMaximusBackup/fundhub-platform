Add Accounts

Add Accounts & Cards

provider: —

Type in a bank account or a credit card and it shows up on the Banking Surface and the money dashboard. Nothing here talks to a real bank. An empty box means "I don't know" — it is left blank on screen as a dash, never turned into a zero.

Pick a client first

This screen needs to know whose accounts you are adding. Open it with a client in the address bar:

banking-entry.html?client_id=<the client's id>

You can get there in one click from a client's page: open the Client Control Panel, find the person, then come back with their id in the address bar.

Fill it with test accounts

Makes a set of pretend accounts — two business, two personal, two credit cards and a brokerage — with balances, spending history and statement dates. The same made-up numbers every time. Safe to press twice: it updates the same accounts instead of adding copies.

Add one by hand

Card billing — leave blank what you don't know.

On file

Interest rates are stored as a fraction between 0 and 1, so 24.99% is kept as 0.2499. Type the percent — the conversion happens once, on the server. Banking Surface · Finance OS · Command Center
