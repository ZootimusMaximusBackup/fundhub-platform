PC-03 / WHY RATES ARE NEVER OVERWRITTEN
A commission rate is a dated fact, not a setting. Changing one closes the current version — its effective-to date is stamped the day before the new rate starts — and opens a new version. Nothing is destroyed. A payout that already accrued keeps pointing at the version that was open the day it was earned, so re-running last quarter's ledger produces last quarter's numbers. Every ledger row below names the exact rule version it was computed from.
