How this works

Write the words once, send them to anyone. A wording holds the parts that never change, plus blanks like {{contact.first_name}} that get filled in with the client's real details, and blanks you type in yourself when you send it.

Look before you send. Press Preview and you see the finished document exactly as the client will. Nothing goes out until you press Send.

The words lock when you send. After that nobody can change them — not you, not anyone. If the stored copy is altered by any route at all, the client's page refuses to accept a signature and says so. That is what makes a signed contract worth something.

You pass the link on yourself. This system does not send email or text messages. Press Send, copy the link, and give it to the client however you already talk to them.
