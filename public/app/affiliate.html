PARTNER TERMS
Rate
first qualified paid outcome on any client attributed to your code, effective 2026-01-15. If the rate ever changes, the version open on the day a client paid is the one that governs that payout.
Attribution window
60 days from first click. Last-touch: if another affiliate's link is clicked after yours inside the window, the later click takes the commission.
Qualifying products
Business Financial Assessment ($32) · Consulting Services Deposit ($3,000) · Consulting Services Package (~$1,000 DIY) · Credit Optimization Bundle ($2,000) · Inquiry Removal. The 10% success fee is not a qualifying product.
Payout schedule
Fridays, ACH, on balances above $50. Below that the balance rolls to the next run.
Clawback
A refund inside 30 days reverses the commission on the next run. After 30 days it stands.
Partner license
Payouts accrue but do not release until the license is signed.
What you can see
Business name, referral date, status, and your own commission. Client contact details, credit data and outcome tiers are outside the Affiliate role.
