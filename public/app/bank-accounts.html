This client's bank accounts, entered by hand, with each one marked personal or business.

Nothing on this page connects to a bank. There is no bank connection in this product — storing bank credentials is behind a security review and a consent flow that are both still open — so every figure here is one a person typed in, and it is only as fresh as the date beside it. A box left empty is stored as unknown and shown as a dash. It is never stored as zero, because “we do not know” and “there is nothing in it” are different facts about somebody's money.
