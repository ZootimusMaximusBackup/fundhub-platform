## The rule this establishes

A 403 from an anti-bot system is not an unreachable host. Try the normal fetch, then curl with
a browser user-agent, then the reader, and only then record it as unreachable — naming which
of the three were tried. `ad-research.js` now carries this as `READER_FALLBACK` and probes
403 hosts through the reader during its Reach phase, so a host like Trustpilot is no longer
written off before the research starts.

Evidence read through the reader stays tier C. You read the page; you just did not read it directly.
