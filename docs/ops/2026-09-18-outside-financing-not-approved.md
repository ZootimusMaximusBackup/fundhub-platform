# Outside financing was not approved — the SLO offer is the funding path now

Date: **2026-09-18**
Source: Chris, direct.
Status: owner-set. Do not re-plan around outside financing.

## What happened

We applied for outside financing to fund growth. **It was not approved.** Some
of the financial planning in this repo was built assuming that money would
arrive. It is not arriving.

## What replaces it

A self-liquidating offer. We sell a **$297 blueprint** on ads, the $297 pays for
the ad that got the buyer, and the buyer becomes a qualified funding lead for
free. Growth is funded out of front-end revenue, not out of a loan.

Model: `docs/finance/slo-offer-model-2026-09-18.md`.

## What this means for future work

* **Do not propose anything that depends on an injection of outside capital.**
  Ad spend scales from collected revenue only.
* **Any older plan, projection, or roadmap in this repo that assumed approved
  financing is dead.** If you find one, say so in your task report. Do not
  quietly build against it.
* The back end is unchanged: $3,000 deposit against a 10% success fee on
  confirmed approvals (`docs/CLOSEOUT-FEE-BASIS.md`).
