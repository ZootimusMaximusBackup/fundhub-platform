FundHub Expected Sequence

What should reach the person, and when, at every step of every path. Read top to bottom next to your phone. Every line is from the live code and the live message table on 2026-09-03; the wording shown is the real subject line or the first words of the text.

Timing rule for everything: a message is queued the instant its trigger fires and leaves on the next dispatcher run, which is every 5 minutes. So "immediately" means within 5 minutes. Texts obey quiet hours (8pm–8am Arizona) unless the address is a +sim- test address, which skips the wait.
