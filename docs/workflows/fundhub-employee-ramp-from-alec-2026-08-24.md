## 1. His one job vs our four seats

Alec hired one **Credit Commando**. That person read reports, fixed credit, ran funding, and sat with the client.

Fundhub does **not** hire Commandos. We hire seats.

| Fundhub seat | What they do | After they graduate, “winning” is |
|--------------|--------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Closer** | Present. Log a **deposit**. | **27 deposits / month** in a pod (closer + funding advisor). Not 20. Twenty is only the camp list. |
| **Funding advisor** | After the close. Prep the file. Run **3–4** funding rounds. | **27 funded files / month** in that same pod. |
| **Inquiry remover** | Wipe inquiries so the file can move. | File clock: healthy about **15 days**, hard stop **30**. No monthly count. Do not invent one. |
| **Credit repair** | Repair work. **Second seat.** Today they share one Specialist login (Inquiries / Repair switch). | Same file clock. Mail is **expedited**, not overnight. No monthly count. |

**Setter is AI.** Count bookings. **Do not hire setters.** Skip his hire-setter and appointment-setter pages. They are empty anyway.

**Sales manager** already exists. They watch the floor. A packed calendar means hire a **closer**, not a setter. They do not sit this Commando camp unless you name a manager camp later.

**Do not copy his pay.** His job post said **$800–$1,200 / month**. That is his Commando pay. It is not Fundhub pay. Our closer / funding-advisor money lives in the employee deal you lock. This file does not set dollars.

## 2. The shape we keep

Every day is the same four boxes. He was clear: reading is not enough.

1. **Homework** — they study  
2. **Concepts** — the one-pagers / videos  
3. **Test** — can they do it on a sample  
4. **Roleplay** — can they do it in front of a person  

No day is done until the test **and** the roleplay are done.

**5 days × 4 boxes = 20 items.**

His tracker page (`5-day-training-camp-tracker--cfe3572f`) is **empty**. It does not list the 20 rows. The 20 come from the camp page (`03-bottom-of-funnel-5-day-training-camp--3b8c3aa7`): four boxes per day.

**Gate (keep):** all **20** checked for **that seat** → they get live people. Anything open → they do not.

His hire kit said about **3 weeks** from first applicant to a person who can produce. Interview first. Then this camp. Then live.

## 3. Before camp — the interview (keep the filter)

Source: `02-middle-of-funnel-the-interview--3b8c3aa7`. His rule: **never process a person you have not seen or heard.**

**Two gates before a call**

1. **Form** — years of work, tools they used, real start date, a short written sample.  
2. **60-second video** — they talk. English and presence. No video → no interview.

**Three scored questions (1–5 each). Do not freestyle.** Average **4 or above** → you. **3 or below** → no or hold.

| # | He asked (Commando) | Fundhub ask (same idea, right seat) |
|---|---------------------|-------------------------------------|
| 1 | A 580 score, two collections, 88% used. What first? | **Closer:** walk a sample Present from a messy file. **Funding advisor:** what do you do in week one after a deposit? **Inquiry:** what do you wipe first, and what do you leave? **Repair:** what do you dispute first, and what do you not promise? |
| 2 | Tell me a time you followed someone else’s written steps. | Same for every seat. We need people who follow **our** screens, not their old shop. |
| 3 | Client is mad they got denied. Next five minutes? | Same for every seat. Listen first. Do not argue. Name the next step and a time. |

**Statuses we can reuse:** Pending → To interview → First screen done → Sent to you → Final done → Hire (offer + NDA + contract). Plus **On hold** and **Declined**.

His job-post kit (`credit-commando-hire-process-job-post-1st-part--345c3aa7` and the hiring-funnel page) is how **he** found Commandos on LinkedIn. Use the **filter**, not the title and not the pay. Post for the **seat** you need (closer, funding advisor, inquiry, repair). When the calendar is full, hire a closer.

## 6. After 20/20 — the week (keep his rhythm, our screens)

He had the new person join the team rhythm on day one after camp. Keep the habit. Use Fundhub, not his Slack-only ritual.

**Each work day**

- **Start:** top 3 jobs, calls on the calendar, what is stuck  
- **End:** what got done, what is stuck, one line per live file  

**Monday — training**  
Red-flag files. Show a new step. Each seat shows their number (deposits, funded files, or file clock).

**Thursday — check**  
Where each file sits. What is blocking funding. What must finish this week.

His manager page (`managing-commandos-1--345c3aa7`) adds three habits we keep: every live file gets a row the day they join. Every job is written down (his P1 / P2 / P3). No “I told them on the call.” We do **not** keep his 60 + 60 day clocks. Ours are about **15** days healthy and **30** hard stop.

His “30+ clients per Commando” is **his** load. Do not copy it. Our load is the pod bars and the file clock.
