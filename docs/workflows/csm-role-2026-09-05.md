## Two things that need Chris, and are NOT decided

### 1. "Halfway" does not match what is built

Chris said the check-in happens **halfway through the service**.

The shipped system fires it **7 days after they pay**
(`MID_DUE_DAYS = 7`, `src/handlers/customer-insights.mjs:12`).

Seven days after payment is not halfway through anything. These are different
calls with different purposes: one is "did we start you off right", the other is
"how is this going". Both are reasonable. They are not the same call.

Agreements carry their own length — the contract text reads *"This agreement
runs for {{field.term_days}} days"* and the standard term is 180 days, so a real
halfway point is computable as `contracts.signed_at + term_days/2`, which is day
90 on a standard agreement.

**Open question for Chris: keep the day-7 call, add a real halfway call at day
90, or move the existing one to day 90?** Nothing has been changed here. This is
recorded as a gap between intended and actual, not quietly reconciled.

### 2. What the CSM is paid on

Chris confirmed the CSM is commissioned. He did not name a number, and rates in
this system are rows with effective dates, never values in code. The capability
now exists (`collections` basis, paid on cash actually received). **No rate is
set.** Until Chris sets one, a CSM earns nothing on collections and the
calculator says so out loud rather than paying zero silently.
