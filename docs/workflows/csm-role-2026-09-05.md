## The headline, in plain words

Chris asked for a new role. Most of that role **was already built and had nobody
assigned to it.** The mid-service check-in, the post interview, the question
lists, the place the answers get stored, and the chase-the-money ladder all
exist and all run today. What was missing was a person to own them, and two
permissions the system could not record.

So this is not a new system. It is one new role plus two guards.

## Two things that need Chris, and are NOT decided

### 1. "Halfway" — DONE, but not the way the plan said

The check-in now fires at **day 90**, not day 7. That is the change Chris asked
for and it has shipped.

**The plan's method was wrong and is recorded here so nobody retries it.** It
said to compute `contracts.signed_at + term_days/2`. That cannot be built:

* `term_days` is a merge value used to render one template's sentence. Nothing
  in `src/` or `api/` ever writes it onto a contract.
* Migration 287 **deliberately removed** how long the work runs from that
  catalogue and moved it into the agreement text Chris supplies. Its own words:
  "Neither was ever a number this catalogue owns."
* Measured on a database with every migration applied: **zero contracts carry a
  term.**

A midpoint computed from data that is not there is a guess with a formula around
it. So it is one number, `MID_DUE_DAYS = 90` in
`src/handlers/customer-insights.mjs` — the midpoint of the 180-day term that is
the only program length stated anywhere in this repo. One place to change it.

### 2. What the CSM is paid — DECIDED

**10% of cash collected, on upsells only. Nothing on collections.**
Owner-set 2026-09-05, shipped as `db/migrations/346_csm_upsell_commission_rate.sql`.

The `collections` basis that 293 opened is **deliberately left empty**. That is
the design, not an omission: a CSM paid to collect leans on the client during a
call that is supposed to be about how the client is doing.

Scoped by role rather than by "upsell motion" because the schema requires a
motion rule to also name one product, and no upsell product list was given. In
practice they are the same thing — closers own new business, so every sale a CSM
is credited on is an upsell. If the products get named later, per-product rules
score higher and simply win over this one.

Corroboration, not authority: 10% is also the only commission figure traceable
to Cole Gordon himself, and his published CSM guidance ties the commission
component to upsells only.
