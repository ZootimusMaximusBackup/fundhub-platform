# W10 — Banking Surface

`status: done` · branch `banking-surface-w10`

**Stacks on two branches, not on `main` alone:** `finance-os-ui-w9` (the Finance
OS screen it extends) and `plaid-banking-w5` (the `bank_accounts` table and the
`entity_kind` column it reads). Both are merged into this branch. Nothing here
works without them.

## What changed, in plain language

A new screen, **Banking Surface**. It lists a client's bank accounts in three
separate cards:

* **Personal** — money we have established is theirs.
* **Business** — money we have established is their company's.
* **Unclassified** — money nobody has placed yet.

**The whole point of the screen is that third card.** An account nobody has
placed is shown on its own, is never added to the client's money, and gets a
banner saying so in words. There is a real temptation to write "personal means
everything that is not business" — one short line — and that line would quietly
tell a closer that a company account, a spouse's account or a trust account is
the client's own cash.

**There is deliberately no single total across the three cards.** One number
spanning personal + business + unclassified gets read as "the client has this
much", and for two of those three that is not established. Its absence is the
feature, and a test asserts the combined figure appears nowhere on the page.

Two more things the screen refuses to smooth over:

* **An overdrawn account shows a negative number, in red.** It is never clamped
  to zero. That is the account somebody most needs to look at.
* **A group total with a hole in it says so** — *"counted 3 of 4 open accounts ·
  1 did not report — this is a floor"*. A bank that did not report a balance
  means unknown, not zero.

## The W5 decision this rests on

W10 could not start until W5 was settled, and there were two opposed W5 PRs:

* **#45** builds a live Plaid client — a real outbound HTTP call in
  `src/adapters/`, carrying a credential that reads a named person's bank
  account.
* **#52** builds the same tables and leaves the provider call as a named, empty
  seam.

**Decided: #52.** Grounds, in order of weight:

1. The autonomy rules for this build say verbatim: *"External call? Seam it.
   Document it. Don't build it yet."*
2. `CLAUDE.md` §12 states "Nothing transmits" as a standing property of the repo.
3. `AUDIT-FINDINGS.md` lists it among four things "OPEN and **deliberate**, not
   oversights", awaiting a provider decision that has not happened.
4. SOC 2 is not done, no `PLAID_*` secret is set anywhere, and `api.netlify.com`
   is blocked by network policy so an operator cannot set one from here.

#45 is good work and should not be discarded — it should land as its own PR
**after** SOC 2 sign-off. Merging it today ships a live bank-credential path into
a product whose stated compliance posture is "not yet".

## Scope — narrowed deliberately

The brief said "extends v1" and named the entity-kind split as the requirement.
It also said to wait for W5–W8. W6, W7 and W8 (card liabilities, bank
transactions and recurring bills, reminders) are **not** included: none is
merged, each has its own competing PRs, and folding four unmerged branches into
one screen would make this unreviewable and would guess at three more decisions
that are not mine. Per the autonomy rules — *"Ambiguous scope? Narrow it. Ship
minimum."*

So this ships the one thing W10 uniquely names: bank accounts, split by whose
money it is, with unclassified kept apart.
