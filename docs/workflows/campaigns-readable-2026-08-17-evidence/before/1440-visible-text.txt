There are four outcomes and no others: done, failed, undone, or written down but never carried out. There is no "pending" and no "retrying" — nothing here tries a second time, so a row that says it was never carried out will stay that way.
Every kill switch row says "never carried out" — that is how it is written down, not what happened. The stop itself did happen: the limit was flagged as breached and the campaigns under it were paused. Ad sets underneath keep the state they had.
The row is written before the change is sent, then stamped when it comes back. That is why "written down but never carried out" is a state you can find rather than a change that vanished. Nothing in this list can be edited or deleted.
"Revertible" is a flag, not a button. Nothing on this screen can undo an action. Rows from the optimiser usually carry no name — the rule is the attribution. Names on human rows are the partner's own users.
