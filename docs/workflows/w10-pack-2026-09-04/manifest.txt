WHY THE WHOLE PACK WAS REPLACED
-------------------------------
The 2026-09-04 pack no longer matched the code, in three ways, and a committed
artefact that contradicts its own write-up is worse than no artefact.

  1. Its LENDER COUNTS were over-promises. It said "6 lenders are open to you
     today" for the sim client and "11 lenders are open to you today" for the
     same client with a company on file. Both counts included lenders that state
     a requirement this system has never checked: Fundbox wants a business bank
     account, Navy Federal wants membership. Nothing in this product records
     either. Unknown is not met. The counts are now 5 and 9, and both lenders
     still appear in the shortlist with their real requirement printed.

  2. Its HARD-CASES roadmap printed "Month 1 - Pay AMEX PLATINUM (NPSL) from
     $5,200 down to under 10% of its limit." — naming a limit the file does not
     have, five pages after the paydown table on the same document printed "-"
     for that same card.

  3. Its HARD-CASES files could not be reproduced from anything in this repo.
     They were printed from a fixture named "Repro Client" that was never
     committed, and the funding snapshot carried the sentence "You do not need to
     lose sleep over these. They are cleanup and upkeep only." — a sentence that
     exists in NO source file. The printer says, and has said,
     "None of these is what your funding decision turns on. They are cleanup and
     upkeep - the note under each one says what, if anything, to do about it."
     (src/underwrite/black-report-node.mjs:584). The fixture now lives in
     scripts/black-reports/regen-w10-pack.mjs, so the pack cannot drift from the
     code again without the command saying so.

`Bank-and-Lender-Match-List.pdf` is gone from this directory. That name came
from the documents table's title slug; the pack builder's own filename is
`Bank-Lender-Match-List.pdf` (src/underwrite/letter-pack-filter.mjs:9). Two
lender lists with two names and two different counts is exactly the confusion
this pack is supposed to prevent.


THE PRINTER, AND HOW WE KNOW WHICH ONE PRODUCTION USES
------------------------------------------------------
Printed by the NODE printer (src/underwrite/black-report-node.mjs), which is
what a real client receives today. Round 3 said so and gave one reason —
"WeasyPrint is not installed on Netlify". That reason is true and it is not the
only one, and the earlier wording read as if the render service were already the
live path. MEASURED 2026-09-06 against the real site:

    netlify env:list --context production --plain | cut -d= -f1

returns 82 names and NEITHER `BLACK_REPORT_RENDER_URL` NOR `FUNDHUB_RENDER_KEY`
is one of them. Same for deploy-preview, branch-deploy and dev. So
resolveRenderService() (src/underwrite/black-report-pdf.mjs:138-143) returns
null on Netlify, the remote step cannot be taken at all, and with no Python on
the Node runtime either, every live print lands on the pdf-lib printer. Run
against that env the resolver says so out loud:

    [black-report] engine=pdf-lib
      reason=render_service_not_configured (local_python_not_attempted)
      files=4 DEGRADED — short documents, not the designed set

Until those two settings exist, the designed WeasyPrint documents are not what
production prints. render-service/README.md step 7 carries the same note.

A laptop with WeasyPrint prints the same five documents through
scripts/black-reports/fundhub_gen.py and the bytes differ — pass
`--engine=python` to produce that set instead. Both printers were run against
the same two fixtures this round and the words they print for the two defects
below are the same words. That path is proved separately in
docs/workflows/w10-proof-2026-09-03.md.

PDFs are not byte-reproducible; every one carries a creation timestamp, so the
cover date moves and the bytes move with it. An unequal hash means the bytes are
new, never that the words moved. Read the file. The sizes below move by a byte or
two between runs for the same reason — they are here to tell one document from
another, not to be diffed.

no-limit/ — NO open card reports a credit limit
------------------------------------------------
NEW IN ROUND 4, and the reason round 4 exists. One open card, an AMEX with no
preset spending limit, and nothing else. The vendor engine sums
`effectiveLimit || 0` (derive-consumer-signals.js:186), so its total limit is 0
and its utilization percentage is null. Ten percent of 0 is 0, and
`balance - 0` is the client's WHOLE balance.
