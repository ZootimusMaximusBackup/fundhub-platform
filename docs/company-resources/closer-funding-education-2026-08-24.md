# COMPLIANCE REVIEW REQUIRED

**This is a teaching sheet for Fundhub closers.** It talks about fees, when money is due, a credit file read, and what happens after someone pays. It is **not** buyer-facing. A human must read it before it goes to staff.

**Never tell a buyer:**

- Their score will go up.
- We will get them funded.
- A bank will give them a set dollar amount.
- A bad mark will come off.
- “0% interest,” “no damage to credit,” or “we protect your score.”

If you cannot explain a path without those lines, stop. Ask a manager. Do not guess.

**For:** Fundhub closers who do not fully understand funding yet

## Owner-set this day (Chris, 2026-08-24)

These are company rules. They win if an older slide or price list clashes.

1. **$3,000 is a good start** for the funding deposit. It is not a wall.
2. Closers **can go UP** on the funding fee as much as the deal supports.
3. Closers **can go LOWER** too. It truly depends.
4. The key is **getting people into funding**.
5. The deposit is **part of** the 10% after they get funded. It is **not** extra on top.
6. About **95%** of people who agree actually pay. About **5%** do not. There is a collection plan for that 5%. The **how-to** for that 5% is marked **UNVERIFIED** below. Do not invent steps.

The listed start in our offer book is still **$3,000**. If you need a different number on the pay link, ask a manager how to set it. Do not invent a button path.

## 1. What “funding” means here

**Funding** means we help a person try to get money from **lenders**. A lender is a bank, a card company, or another money company that can say yes or no.

**We are not the bank.** We do not hand them a pile of cash from Fundhub’s pocket. A lender decides. We do the work around that decision.

What “we do the work” means on the done-for-you path:

- We **match** them to lenders that fit the file we can see.
- We **plan rounds** (groups of applications, not a spray).
- We help with the **apply** steps on those rounds.
- We have a person whose job is **inquiry work** between rounds (a hard look on a credit file is called an inquiry).

That scope is what the funding agreement wording is built for: match, plan rounds, inquiry sweeps between rounds. It is **not** a promise a lender will say yes.

**Lender-of-record** (who the law treats as the lender): **UNVERIFIED** in this repo for the done-for-you path. Do not tell a buyer we are the lender. Do not tell them we are “not the lender of record” either. Say: **lenders decide. We help you apply on a plan.**

The **courses** are different. Course terms say those programs are **school**, not lending, and not credit repair. A course buyer learns the map. We do not apply for them on a course.

**They do not need a business.** Personal is fine. If they ask “do I need an LLC first?” the Fundhub answer is no. That is our rule. Another shop may have required a business. We do not.

## 2. Why a $32 file read exists vs the done-for-you offer

Two different products. Do not mix them.

### The $32 file read (soft pull)

**Soft pull** means a **soft look** at the credit file. It is not us sending a bank application.

| Fact | What to know |
|---|---|
| Price | **$32**, cash only |
| Name in the offer book | UnderwriteIQ soft-pull assessment |
| Job | See the **real** file, not the score they typed on a form |
| What it is not | Not a bank app. Not the $3,000 funding start. Not “you’re approved.” |

They must **pay** and **consent** before we pull. A typed score is a guess until we have a real look. Do not pull before they pay and agree.

Why it exists:

- Some people are a **maybe**. The honest next step is to look, then pick a real path — or say we are not a fit.
- If they said they have **negatives** (late pays, collections, other bad marks), even a 700+ person may need a look first.
- You may use the $32 read on **any** lane when the file is unclear.

Do **not** say “this will not affect you at all” as a blanket promise. Do **not** say “once we pull, you’ll be approved.”

### The done-for-you funding offer

This is us **doing the funding work** with them.

| Fact | What to know |
|---|---|
| Usual start | **$3,000** cash now (a good start — can move; see owner-set) |
| Later | **10%** of money a lender later funds, **if** a lender funds them |
| How they pay the start | **Cash only.** They cannot put this on Commas (our checkout / payments partner) |
| Usual buyer | Score about **700 or higher**, and they can pay the start in cash |
| Business? | **Not required** |

Say the work. Do not say the win. “We match, we plan rounds, we work inquiries between rounds. Lenders decide.”

## 3. Who is a funding buyer vs a course buyer vs a cash downsell

Pick the lane from what they told us (score they typed, cash they said they have, what they want). The $32 read is how we see the **real** file later. Do not treat a typed score as hard fact.

| Lane | Who | What they hear | What they do **not** hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Funding** | About **700+**, and they can pay the start in cash | Done-for-you funding (start fee + 10% later) | Do not push a course as the main close if they are a cash funding buyer |
| **Funding + repair** | About **700+**, cash for about **$4,000** ($3,000 + $1,000), and the file looks like it needs work | Funding start **plus** full repair. Same 10% later if a lender funds them | Do not promise repair will raise the score so funding “will work” |
| **Course finance** | About **500+**, not a usual cash funding buyer (under 700, **or** 700+ with almost no cash) | UnderwriteIQ pack and/or Funding Mastery on **Commas** if they qualify | Do not finance funding. Do not finance repair |
| **Cash downsell** | Under 500, “not sure” on score, or they will not / cannot finance | Soft pull **$32**, repair trial **$200**, full repair **$1,000** — cash | Do not force the $3,000. Do not invent a payment plan for cash offers |

### Extra gates (plain words)

- **No business required.** Personal is fine.
- **Negatives on the file:** they may not be the usual funding buyer even at 700+. Look first. The $32 read is the honest step.
- **700–749 with under $1,000 cash:** they are **not** a $3,000 buyer today. Course finance (if about 500+) or a cash downsell.
- **Small ask:** still listen. Do not cancel someone because they want a smaller amount of money later. That cancel rule belonged to another shop with a much bigger start fee. It is **not** our rule.

### The two courses (the only things that can finance)

Chris locked this: **only two things** can go on financing (Commas).

1. **UnderwriteIQ pack** — listed **$1,000**, can go up to **$5,000**
2. **Funding Mastery** — **$5,000**

Course finance rules Chris set:

- Score they can show is about **500 or higher**
- Term is about **12 to 24 months**
- Rate Chris said: about **20%**. The offer book does **not** store that rate. Treat **~20%** as owner-said, not a number you invent on a call if they ask for a locked APR. If they need the exact checkout rate, that is **Commas**, not you.
- If they say **“not sure”** on score, they **cannot** finance. Cash, or they do not buy the course.

**Everything else is cash:** $32, $200 trial, $1,000 repair, funding start, $4,000 combo. An older sales file may still show repair as “finance available.” **Ignore that.** Courses only.

A course is **not** us getting them funded. It is them learning the map.

## 4. The money: pay now vs 10% later

Two moments. Do not mash them together.

**Pay now** = the start fee so we can begin (the deposit).  
**Pay later** = the **rest** of 10% **if** a lender funds them.

### The deposit is part of the 10%

This is the line people get wrong.

> They pay the start now so we can start. That money **counts toward** the 10%. If a lender later funds them, they pay the **rest** of the 10%. They do **not** pay 10% plus the start.

**Example only. Not a promise they will get $75,000.**

- If they were funded for **$75,000**, 10% is **$7,500**.
- They already paid **$3,000**.
- Rest due later: **$4,500**.

If you used a different start (higher or lower), the same idea holds: start money counts toward the 10%. The rest is 10% minus what they already paid.

If 10% would be **less** than what they already paid, do **not** invent a refund or a “bonus back.” If they ask, stop and ask a manager.

The funding agreement wording we send uses: success fee = **10% of funded amount**, due **within 7 days of funding**. The 10% is figured from the **funded amount** on the round (the money they actually got / we bill against), not from a guess on the sales call.

### The start fee can move

Owner-set this day:

- **$3,000 is a good start.** Not a wall.
- You **can go up** if the deal supports it.
- You **can go lower** if that is what gets a real person into funding.
- The key is **getting people into funding.**

Do not turn “can move” into a fake payment plan. Funding start is still **cash**. You are changing the **size** of the start, not putting it on Commas.

### What has no 10%

Repair, the $200 trial, both courses, and the $32 read have **no** success fee. Only the funding path has the 10% later.

A closer who understands funding still loses if they sell a fantasy.

| Never | Why |
|---|---|

| “Your score will go up.” | Banned. We do not promise score moves. |

| “0% interest” / “this isn’t really debt” | It can be debt. Rates are the lender’s. |
| “No damage to credit” / “we protect your score” | You cannot keep that. |

Some Present slides still talk like a sure path or a fast dollar. **You still may not promise a fund.** If a slide over-sells, stay with the honest line: work we do, lenders decide, no score promise.

## 7. What happens after they say yes

Only what we can trace. If a step is missing in the repo, it is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

### On the call (you)

1. Name **one** offer in dollars. Cash vs finance.
2. Ask for the yes.
3. Open **Present** (the screen you share) when you show the plan.
4. Send the **agreement** that matches the offer (funding agreement, combo, repair, or the $32 consent).
5. Send the **pay link**. Present’s Pay action emails and texts the pay link and asks them to stay on the Meet.
6. **Stay on** until they pay, they need a minute with a real next time, or they say no.

The closer pack is the source for that order. Send **both** the agreement and the pay link. Do not smash Present / pay / pull. Soft pull is $32 and needs their yes and the right consent.

If they paid: they become a **client**. You are not their forever handler. Hand off clean.

### After the start fee clears (funding path)

When the deposit payment lands, the system marks them a **Funding Client** and asks for **documents**. Funding work waits on those docs until the checker accepts them.

What we can name from the repo:

- They get tagged as a funding client.
- They get a **document request** (email + text). Next action: collect documents.
- There is a task to **pull the credit file** (CRS — the credit host we use). That intake task is assigned to the **closer** seat in the workflow we found.
- The **funding advisor** is the next working seat. They run the first **client-start** call after pay: file, portal, next steps. Inquiry or repair joins if the file needs them.
- A later event (`round.started`) is when a funding **round** actually begins. If no pod is assigned yet, a task goes to the funding advisor: assign pod roles.

**Exact minute-by-minute “who calls the client first after pay”:** the ramp doc says the **funding advisor** runs that first working call. The post-pay workflow we traced also makes a **closer** intake task to pull CRS. If those two clash on a live file, ask a manager. Do not invent a third seat.

### After docs are in (funding path)

Traced belt, in plain words:

1. Docs accepted → the hold can lift.
2. A **round** starts (apply now).
3. Round submitted → inquiry work can sit between rounds.
4. If a lender **approves**, they may still have more steps (money is not always in yet).
5. If a round is **funded**, the rest of the 10% can be invoiced. Client mail can say the fee is locked **only if** the amount and the percent are both on the file.
6. If they do not pay that **later** 10% invoice, **accounts-receivable chase** exists (see below). That chase is for the **success fee after funding**, not for the 5% who never paid the start.

**How many rounds, how many weeks, which lenders:** do not invent a lender list or an APR. The employee ramp teaches “about 3–4 rounds” and “prep is days, not months” as **training shape**, not a buyer promise. Do not tell a buyer “two weeks to funded.”

### The 5% who agree but do not pay the start

**Owner-set:** about **95%** who agree pay. About **5%** do not. Chris said there **is** a collection plan for that 5%.

What the repo actually has (do not treat this as a full script):

| What exists | What it is | What it is **not** |
|---|---|---|
| Stay on the call until they pay | Closer pack. First move. | Not a later chase |
| A task “Yes — send contract + collect payment” | Fires if they text YES after the call in that inbound path | Not a full 5% playbook |
| **Unsigned contract** reminders | After **3 days** unsigned, remind again every **3 days**, up to **4** reminders, plus a staff task | This is for people who **did not sign**. Not proven as the 5% “signed / agreed, never paid” path |
| **Success-fee** invoice chase | After they **get funded** and the 10% invoice is sent: notice now, reminder at **+7 days**, final at **+14 days**, then a handoff tag. **No fourth client message.** Stops if they pay | This is **not** the 5% who never paid the $3,000 start |

**How-to for the 5% unpaid start fee: UNVERIFIED.**  
Do not invent a collections script, a legal threat, a discount, or a “day 10 send to legal” line for people who only agreed on a call. If you have a live 5% file, ask a manager. Use only a plan Chris has given you in writing.

## Can you explain it? (self-check)

Say these out loud to a peer. If you slip into a guarantee, you are not ready.

1. **What is funding here?** We help them try to get money from lenders. We are not the bank. Lenders decide.
2. **Why $32?** To see the real file. Soft look. Not a bank app. Not the done-for-you offer.
3. **Who hears funding vs a course vs $32 / $200 / $1,000?** 700+ with cash → funding. 500+ who are not a cash funding buyer → courses (financeable). Others → cash downsells. No business required.
4. **How does the money work?** Start now, rest of 10% later if they get funded. Start **counts toward** the 10%. $3,000 is a good start; the number can go up or down. Courses only on Commas.
5. **What do you never promise?** Score up. Sure fund. A set dollar from a bank.
6. **What happens after yes?** Agreement + pay link. Stay on. After they pay, docs and the funding advisor. You hand off. You do not invent the 5% chase.
