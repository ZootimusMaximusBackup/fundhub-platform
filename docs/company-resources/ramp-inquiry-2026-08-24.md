## Day 1 — See inquiries

Funding rounds add looks on the file. Your job is those looks (and personal-info errors), not the whole repair pack.

**Quiz**

1. What is an inquiry in plain words?  
2. What two things do you mark first on a report?  
3. Do you promise the next round will fund?

**Trainer key:** 1) A hard look a lender or other company made. 2) Inquiries + personal-info errors. 3) No.

## Day 3 — Wipe vs leave

Wipe **between** rounds so the funding advisor can run the next round on a cleaner look-list. Do not invent which brands to wipe if the written rule is not in front of you. Ask.

**Quiz**

1. When do you usually wipe — during a round or between?  
2. What if you are not sure leave vs wipe?  
3. Is there a monthly wipe count?

**Trainer key:** 1) Between rounds. 2) Ask a manager. Write it down. 3) No.

## Day 5 — Graduation

Stuck bureau work: show only if this role may see it. Confirming an answer is a click.

**Day 5 quiz (must miss zero)**

1. Where do you sit in the CRM?  
2. When do you wipe vs leave?
