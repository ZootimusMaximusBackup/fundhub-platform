# Objections Sheet + Funding Space Guide

**For:** Fundhub sales manager and the closers you run
**Date:** 2026-09-01
**Internal only.** Do not send this to a buyer.

This is two things in one place. **Part 1** is the funding space — what we actually sell, how the money works, and who fits which lane. **Part 2** is the objections sheet — eighteen objections with word-for-word answers.

A closer who cannot pass the self-check at the end of Part 1 is not ready for a live call.

---

## The five lines that get people fired

Never say these. Not as a joke, not as a "well technically," not to save a deal.

- "Your score will go up."
- "We will get you funded."
- A dollar amount a bank "will" give them.
- A bad item "will" come off.
- "0% interest," "no damage to credit," or "we protect your score."

Also banned: a made-up win, client count, or story. Only wins Chris has given you **in writing**.

If a closer cannot explain a path without those lines, they stop and ask you. They do not guess.

# PART 1 — THE FUNDING SPACE

## 1. What "funding" actually means here

**Funding** means we help a person try to get money from **lenders**. A lender is a bank, a card company, or another money company that can say yes or no.

**We are not the bank.** We do not hand anyone cash out of Fundhub's pocket. A lender decides. We do the work around that decision.

What "we do the work" means on the done-for-you path:

- We **match** them to lenders that fit the file we can see.
- We **plan rounds** — groups of applications, not a spray.
- We help with the **apply** steps on those rounds.
- We have a person whose only job is **inquiry work** between rounds. (A hard look at a credit file is called an inquiry.)

That is the whole scope: match, plan rounds, inquiry sweeps between rounds. It is **not** a promise a lender will say yes.

**Do not tell a buyer we are the lender.** Do not tell them we are "not the lender of record" either. The line is: **lenders decide, we help you apply on a plan.**

The **courses** are a different animal. Course terms say those programs are **school** — not lending, not credit repair. A course buyer learns the map. We do not apply for them on a course.

**They do not need a business.** Personal is fine. If they ask "do I need an LLC first?" the answer is no. Other shops require a business. We do not.

## 3. The funding deposit — owner-set

- **$3,000 is a good start.** It is not a wall.
- Closers **can go up** on the funding fee as much as the deal supports.
- Closers **can go lower** too.
- It **truly depends** on the person and the deal.
- The job is **getting people into funding.** Do not lose a fundable buyer over a rigid $3,000.
- Whatever they pay now — $3,000 or another cash number — is still **part of** the 10% success fee. It is not extra. Nobody invents new 10% math.
- About **95%** of people who agree to pay actually pay. About **5%** do not. See section 8.

Write the real dollar on the contract and the pay link. Never say one number on the call and send another.

The deposit is **cash now.** It cannot go on Commas (our finance plan). Going lower is still cash. Going higher is still cash. **There is no funding payment plan.**

---

## 4. How the deposit and the 10% fit together

This is the line people get wrong most often.

The deposit is **part of the 10%.** It is not extra on top.

Say it like this — swap in the real dollar they will pay now:

> "You pay [amount] now so we can start. That money counts toward the 10%. If a lender later funds you, you pay the rest of the 10%. You do not pay 10% plus that deposit."

Math a closer can say out loud. This is an **example**, not a promise they will get $75,000:

- Funded for **$75,000** → 10% is **$7,500**
- Already paid **$3,000** → rest due later: **$4,500**
- Already paid **$2,000** → rest due later: **$5,500**
- Already paid **$5,000** → rest due later: **$2,500**

The funding agreement wording says: success fee = **10% of funded amount**, due **within 7 days of funding**. The 10% is figured from the **funded amount on the round** — the money they actually got — not from a guess on the sales call.

If 10% would be **less** than what they already paid, nobody invents a refund or a "bonus back." If they ask about refunds, the closer stops and asks you.

**What has no 10%:** repair, the $200 trial, both courses, and the $32 read. Only the funding path carries the 10% later.

## 5. What can be financed

Only **two** things can go on financing (Commas):

1. **UnderwriteIQ pack**
2. **Funding Mastery**

Rules for that financing:

- Score they can show is about **500 or higher**
- Term is about **12 to 24 months**
- Rate is about **20%** — this is owner-said, not a locked APR a closer quotes. If they need the exact checkout rate, that is Commas, not the closer.
- If they say **"not sure"** on score, they **cannot** finance. Cash, or they do not buy the course.

**Everything else is cash.** That includes the $32, the $200, the $1,000 repair, the funding deposit at any size, and the funding + $1,000 combo.

A course is **not** us getting them funded. It is them learning the map.

## 6. Why the $32 file read exists

Two different products. Do not let closers mix them.

**Soft pull** means a **soft look** at the credit file. It is not us sending a bank application.

| Fact | What to know |
|---|---|
| Price | **$32**, cash only |
| Name in the offer book | UnderwriteIQ soft-pull assessment |
| Job | See the **real** file, not the score they typed on a form |
| What it is not | Not a bank app. Not the funding start. Not "you're approved." |

They must **pay** and **consent** before we pull. A typed score is a guess until we have a real look.

Why it exists:

- Some people are a **maybe.** The honest next step is to look, then pick a real path — or say we are not a fit.
- If they said they have **negatives** (late pays, collections, other bad marks), even a 700+ person may need a look first.
- It can be used on **any** lane when the file is unclear.

Never say "this will not affect you at all" as a blanket promise. Never say "once we pull, you'll be approved."

## 7. Who sits on which lane

Pick the lane from what they told us — score they typed, cash they said they have, what they want. A typed score is not a hard fact.

| Lane | Who | What you sell | What you do **not** sell |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Funding** | About **700+**, and they can pay a **cash deposit** that fits this deal | Funding (deposit now + 10% later) | Do not push a course as the main close if they are a cash funding buyer. |
| **Funding + repair** | About **700+**, cash for **deposit + $1,000**, and the file looks like it needs work | Deposit + $1,000 | Do not promise the repair will raise the score so funding "will work." |
| **Course finance** | About **500+**, not a usual funding-cash buyer (under 700, or 700+ with almost no cash) | UnderwriteIQ pack and/or Funding Mastery on Commas | Do not finance funding. Do not finance repair. |
| **Cash downsell** | Under 500, "not sure," or they will not / cannot finance | Soft pull $32, repair trial $200, full repair $1,000 — cash | Do not force a funding deposit. Do not invent a payment plan. |

**Extra gates, plain words:**

- **No business required.** Personal is fine.
- **Negatives on the file** — they may not be the usual funding buyer even at 700+. Look first. The $32 read is the honest step.
- **700–749 with under $1,000 cash** — not a $3,000 buyer today. Course finance (if about 500+) or a cash downsell.
- **Almost no cash at 700+** is not an automatic no. If a **lower** cash deposit still fits, use it. Do not lose a fundable buyer over a rigid number.
- **Small ask** — still listen. Do not cancel someone because they want a smaller amount later. That cancel rule belonged to a shop with a much bigger start fee. It is not ours.

## 8. What happens after they say yes

**On the call:**

1. Name **one** offer in dollars. Cash vs finance.
2. Ask for the yes.
3. Open **Present** (the shared screen) when showing the plan.
4. Send the **agreement** that matches the offer.
5. Send the **pay link** for the **same** dollar just named.
6. **Stay on** until they pay, they need a minute with a real next time, or they say no.

Send **both** the agreement and the pay link. Do not smash Present / pay / pull. The soft pull is $32 and needs their yes and the right consent.

**After the start fee clears (funding path):**

- They get tagged as a **funding client**.
- They get a **document request** by email and text. Funding work waits on those docs.
- A task fires to **pull the credit file**.
- The **funding advisor** is the next working seat. They run the first client-start call: file, portal, next steps. Inquiry or repair joins if the file needs them.
- A funding **round** begins later.

The closer is not the forever handler. Hand off clean.

**After docs are in:**

1. Docs accepted → the hold lifts.
2. A **round** starts.
3. Round submitted → inquiry work sits between rounds.
4. A lender **approves** — they may still have more steps. Money is not always in yet.
5. A round is **funded** → the rest of the 10% can be invoiced.

**Do not invent a lender list, an APR, or a timeline.** Training shape is "about 3–4 rounds" and "prep is days, not months." That is for staff, not a buyer promise. Nobody tells a buyer "two weeks to funded."

**The 5% who agree and do not pay the start:** collections exist for them. There is **no approved closer script** for this. Do not let a closer invent one, threaten, or make up a deadline. Bring that file to you.

## 9. Self-check — can they explain it?

Have every closer say these out loud to you. If they slip into a guarantee, they are not ready for a live book.

1. **What is funding here?** We help them try to get money from lenders. We are not the bank. Lenders decide.
2. **Why $32?** To see the real file. Soft look. Not a bank app. Not the done-for-you offer.
3. **Who hears funding vs a course vs a cash downsell?** 700+ with cash → funding. 500+ who are not a cash funding buyer → courses (financeable). Others → cash downsells. No business required.
4. **How does the money work?** Start now, rest of 10% later if they get funded. Start **counts toward** the 10%. $3,000 is a good start; the number can move. Courses only on Commas.
5. **What do you never promise?** Score up. Sure fund. A set dollar from a bank.
6. **What happens after yes?** Agreement + pay link. Stay on. After they pay, docs and the funding advisor. Hand off. Do not invent the 5% chase.

# PART 2 — THE OBJECTIONS SHEET

**The shape: Align → Educate → Reframe → Close.**

An objection is usually a question. Listen. Ask one follow-up. Do not argue.

After you align, you may **not** "educate" with a fake guarantee. The reframe has to be true at our prices.

---

### "I don't want a bunch of inquiries."

- **Align:** "You should care about that. Extra looks can pile up."
- **Educate:** "The $32 read is a soft look at the file, not a bank app. If you buy funding, apps come later, on a plan, not as a spray. We also have a person whose job is inquiry work. I will not promise a number of looks or that your score stays the same."
- **Close:** "Want to start with the $32 read so we only talk apps after we've seen the file?"
- **Banned:** "1–2 inquiries max." "We protect your score."

---

### "I already applied and got told no."

- **Align:** "That's frustrating. A no stays in your head."
- **Educate:** "A past no is a fact. It does not tell me this file can or cannot get a later yes. I will not promise we flip it."
- **Close:** "If you want a true next step, we read the file for $32 and I tell you if we even have a path — funding, repair, a course, or none."

---

### "I don't want to personally guarantee anything."

- **Align:** "I hear you. People do not like their name on the hook."
- **Educate:** "Banks set their own rules. I will not promise you can skip a guarantee."
- **Close:** "If a guarantee is a hard no for you, say so. We may not be the funding path. We can still talk a course or a file read."
- **Banned:** the "training wheels, then we take the PG off" story.

---

### "I don't have a business." / "Why would I need business credit?"

- **Answer:** "You do not need a business to work with us."
- **Banned:** the "wealthy people never use personal credit" pitch.

---

### "I've been burned by a funding company."

- **Align:** "That is a fair reason to go slow."
- **Educate:** "I will show you the fee in dollars before you pay. Funding starts with a cash deposit — $3,000 is a good start — and that money is part of 10% later if a lender funds you. You get a portal. You get a team after you pay. I will not use a fake client count."
- **Close:** "What would you need to see *in writing* on the agreement before you'd start small — even at $32?"

---

### "This sounds too good to be true."

- **Align:** "If someone promised you a huge number and 'no risk,' you should doubt it."
- **Educate:** "I am not promising a fund. I am telling you our prices. Funding starts with a cash deposit, not $8,000. Courses can be financed. Everything else is cash."
- **Close:** "Which part sounds too smooth — the price, the work, or the 'if' on the lender?"
- **Banned:** "0% interest is real."

---

### "How do I know you won't take my money and vanish?"

- **Align:** "People have been left hanging. I get why you ask."
- **Educate:** "After you pay, you get the portal and the next seat — funding advisor or repair — not just me."
- **Close:** "Want me to walk the agreement on screen so you see what you are paying for before the link?"
- **Banned:** quoting a made-up "3,000 clients."

---

### "Can you prove this works?"

- **Align:** "You should not buy on a vibe."
- **Educate:** "I can only use wins Chris has put in writing for us. If I do not have one that matches you, I will say I don't have it. I will not invent a screenshot."
- **Close:** "If we do not have a matching win today, the honest proof step is the $32 file read or the $200 trial — still no result promise."

---

### "What if I can't pay it back?"

- **Align:** "That is the right fear to have."
- **Educate:** "If a lender gives you a line or a loan, their rules are yours. I will not call that 'not really debt.' If you cannot take on a new line, I will not sell you funding."
- **Close:** "Should we stop at a file read or a course, and leave funding off the table?"
- **Banned:** "business credit doesn't carry the same pressure." "It doesn't show on personal."

---

### "I already have cards / lines."

- **Align:** "Then you already have some doors open."
- **Educate:** "We do not have to stack more. If what you have covers the goal, you may not need us."
- **Close:** "Is the gap 'more room,' 'better terms,' or 'the file is a mess'? Only the last two might be us — and still no promise."

---

### "I don't want more debt."

- **Align:** "Then I will not talk you into funding."
- **Educate:** "Funding is asking a lender for more room. A course is paying to learn. Repair is paying for file work. None of those is 'free money.'"
- **Close:** "Which of those three, if any, do you actually want?"

---

### "I'd rather use my own cash / profits."

- **Align:** "Using your own money is a real choice."
- **Educate:** "Then you may not want the funding path. A $32 read or a course can still be useful. I will not shame you for not borrowing."
- **Note:** the "let the banks fund your next moves" push is allowed **only** if they already said they want a lender. Never use it to create want.

---

### "I need to think about it."

- **Align:** "You should understand it before you pay."
- **Educate:** "What most people need is not more days. It's the one unclear piece. What's the piece?"
- **Close:** "If the unclear piece is the file, $32 answers it. If it's the fee, I'll restate the deposit-in-the-10% math. If it's trust, we stop today and you keep the recap."
- **Banned as pressure:** "missed momentum." "Banks will tighten."

---

### "I'll do it later / next quarter."

- **Align:** "Later is allowed."
- **Educate:** "I will not book a fake urgent slot. If later is real, I'll send a short recap. If a small step would help you decide, that's the $32 read."
- **Note:** do not cancel someone for "wants funding in six months." You are already on the call. Be honest. Do not force a deposit today.

---

### "$3,000 is a lot." / "Can I finance the deposit?"

- **Align:** "It is real money. I will not pretend it isn't."
- **Educate:** "The funding start is cash. We cannot put funding on Commas. Only the two courses finance. $3,000 is a good start, not a wall — we can go lower or higher if this deal supports it. Whatever you pay now is not extra on top of 10%. It counts toward it."
- **Close:** "If no cash deposit fits, we look at a course (if about 500+) or $32 / $200 / $1,000 cash. We do not invent a funding payment plan."

---

### "Other companies want $8,000 or $10,000."

- **Answer:** "That is their price. Ours for funding is a cash deposit now — $3,000 is a good start — plus 10% later if a lender funds you. I will not sell their package. I will not match a made-up middle number."

---

### "Can I finance repair or the trial?"

- **Answer:** "No. Repair and the trial are cash. Financing is only the UnderwriteIQ pack and Funding Mastery."

---

### "What if you don't get me funded?"

- **Answer:** "I cannot promise a fund. I will not promise a refund unless a manager has told you a written rule. If you need a guarantee of money, I will not take your deposit."

# THE POCKET CARD

## Always say

- What they pay **now**, in dollars.
- Cash vs finance, and that **only two courses** finance.
- The deposit **is part of** the 10%.
- "$3,000 is a good start" — not a wall — when you talk funding cash.
- "Lenders decide."
- "We do not promise the score moves."
- "You do not need a business."
- "No" if they are not a fit.

## Never say

| Never | Why |
|---|---|
| "We'll get you funded." | You cannot know that. |
| "Your score will go up." | Banned claim. |
| "0% interest business credit." | Not ours. |
| "$50K–$250K." | A competitor's range. Not a Fundhub promise. |
| "$8,000" or "$10,000" as our price | Not our offer. |
| "We protect your score." / "1–2 inquiries max." | A promise you cannot keep. |
| "Negatives off in five days." | Not our claim. |
| "Overnight letters." | We send **expedited** US mail. Never UPS or FedEx to a bureau. |
| "Just checking in." | Sounds weak. Use a real recap. |
| Made-up wins, "thousands of clients," screenshots you do not have | A lie. |
| "This isn't really debt." | It can be. |
| "You must have an LLC / aged corp." | Not our rule. |
| Taking their incorporation date as fact | It must be verified. The form date is their claim. |
| "I can finance the deposit / the repair." | False. |
| "The deposit is extra on top of 10%." | False. |

# THE FOUR DRILLS

Make every closer say these out loud until they are boring.

1. **"$3,000 is a good start, not a wall. Whatever you pay now is part of the 10%."** Then run the $75,000 example, then say "example, not a promise."
2. **"I cannot promise a lender will say yes."**
3. **"I cannot promise the score will move."**
4. **"We cannot finance the funding deposit. We can talk a course if you're about 500+, or a $32 / $200 / $1,000 cash step."**

If a closer cannot say those four without slipping into a guarantee, they are not ready for a live book.

# AFTER EVERY CALL

The recap they send must include:

- The offer they named, or "no fit"
- The dollar amount — the real one, not a guess
- Cash vs finance
- The 10% math, if it was funding
- Contract and pay link, if they sent them
- One next time, if the buyer asked to think

**Never in a recap:** "just checking in," "are you still interested?", or a new price that was not said on the call.

## Their own review, same day

After every call the closer writes:

1. Three things to **keep**
2. Three things to **change** next time
3. The real reason the buyer bought, stayed, or left — in the buyer's words, not theirs

Review wins as well as losses. And mark this every time: **did I promise a score or a fund?** If yes, that is a miss even if they paid.
