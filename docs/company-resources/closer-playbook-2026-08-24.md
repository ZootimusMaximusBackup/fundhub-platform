## 3. How the deposit and the 10% fit

**[Fundhub]** The deposit is **part of the 10%**. It is not extra on top.

Say it like this (swap in the real dollar they will pay now):

> “You pay [amount] now so we can start. That money counts toward the 10%. If a lender later funds you, you pay the rest of the 10%. You do not pay 10% plus that deposit.”

Math you can say out loud (this is an **example**, not a promise they will get $75,000):

- If they were funded for **$75,000**, 10% is **$7,500**.
- If they already paid **$3,000**, rest due later: **$4,500**.
- If they already paid **$2,000**, rest due later: **$5,500**.
- If they already paid **$5,000**, rest due later: **$2,500**.
