## Day 1 — Read the file

**Study.** Funding means we help a person try to get money from **lenders**. We do not hand them cash from our pocket. They do **not** need a business.

The **$32** read is a soft look at the real file. Not a bank app. Not the done-for-you start.

On a report, mark:

- Late pays  
- How full the cards are  
- Inquiries (hard looks)  
- Bad marks  
- Wrong personal info  

**Quiz (trainer key at the bottom of this day)**

1. Are we the bank?  
2. Do they need an LLC first?  
3. What is the $32 for?  
4. Name four things you mark on a report.

**Trainer key:** 1) No. Lenders decide. 2) No. 3) Soft look at the real file after they pay and consent. 4) Lates, full cards, inquiries, bad marks (personal-info errors also count).

## Day 2 — All products + the CRM

**Study.** Only these sells (from the offer book + owner rules this week). We close **courses** too, not only funding.

| Offer | Pay now | How they pay |
|-------|---------|--------------|
| Soft pull | $32 | Cash |
| Funding, we do it | About **$3,000** start (can move) + 10% later if a lender funds them | Cash. Not Commas. |
| Funding + repair | That start + $1,000 | Cash |
| Full repair | $1,000 | Cash (ignore old “finance repair” notes) |
| Repair trial | $200 | Cash |
| UnderwriteIQ pack | $1,000, can go up to $5,000 | **Can finance** if they qualify (~500+) |
| Funding Mastery | $5,000 | **Can finance** if they qualify (~500+) |

Usual funding buyer: about **700+** and they can pay the start in cash. Course buyer: about **500+** and not a cash funding buyer.

**$3,000 is a good start, not a wall.** You can go up. You can go lower. Write the real dollar on the contract and the pay link.

Example only (not a promise they get $75,000): funded $75,000 → 10% is $7,500 → they already paid $3,000 → rest $4,500.

About **95%** who agree pay. About **5%** do not. **How you chase that 5% is UNVERIFIED.** Do not invent a script. Ask a manager.

**Quiz**

1. Is the $3,000 extra on top of the 10%?  
2. Can they put the funding start on Commas?  
3. What two things can finance?  
4. What does “deposit logged” mean in one line?

**Trainer key:** 1) No. It counts toward the 10%. 2) No. 3) UnderwriteIQ pack and Funding Mastery. 4) They paid the start. They are a client. You hand off. You do not become their forever handler.

## Day 3 — Hard talks

**Study.** You still lose if you sell a fantasy.

Never: sure fund, score up, a set bank dollar, 0% / no damage, overnight letters, finance the $3,000, made-up wins.

If they are mad they got denied later: listen first. Do not argue. Name the next step and a time. Get a manager if you want to invent a fix.

**Quiz**

1. Client: “So you’ll get me the money?” What do you say?  
2. Client: “Will my score go up?” What do you say?  
3. When do you stop and get a manager?

**Trainer key:** 1) We help you apply on a plan. Lenders decide. I cannot promise a yes. 2) We do not promise score moves. 3) Refunds, 10% less than the start, 5% unpaid start, anything not in the playbook.

---

## Day 4 — After the close (you do not run funding)

**Study.** After they pay: documents, then the **funding advisor** runs the first working call. About **3–4** rounds. Inquiry wipe can sit **between** rounds. Prep is days, not months. Do not tell a buyer “two weeks to funded.”

You do **not** run a live card app in this camp. You only explain the belt without over-selling.

**Quiz**

1. Who runs the first working call after pay?  
2. How many rounds do we teach as shape (not a buyer promise)?  
3. May you promise a fund date?

**Trainer key:** 1) Funding advisor. 2) About 3–4. 3) No.

---

## Day 5 — Graduation

**Study.** On the call: one offer, ask for the yes, **Present**, send the **agreement** that matches, send the **pay link**, **stay on**. Soft pull is $32 and needs pay + consent. Do not smash Present / pay / pull.

**Day 5 quiz (must miss zero)**

1. What is funding here?  
2. Why does $32 exist?  
3. Who hears funding vs a course vs cash downsell?  
4. How do the start and the 10% fit?  
5. Name three things you never promise.

**Trainer key:** Match funding education “Can you explain it?” Same answers. If they slip into a guarantee, they are not ready.

**Box 20:** Recorded mock Present / close. Grade: honest lane, right offer, no banned line, Present + agreement + pay link named, stay-on named. Then hand reports to Sarah.
