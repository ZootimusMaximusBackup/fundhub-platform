## Day 1 — Read the file like they just paid

You inherit a closer’s yes. Read the file so the first working call is honest.

Know: lates, full cards, inquiries, bad marks, wrong personal info.

Funding = match, plan rounds, help apply, inquiry work between rounds. **Not** a promise a lender says yes.

**Quiz**

1. Did they already pay the start? What does that make them?  
2. Are we the lender?  
3. Do they need a business?

**Trainer key:** 1) Yes (in this camp story). They are a client. 2) Do not say we are or are not “lender of record.” Say lenders decide; we help apply on a plan. 3) No.

---

## Day 2 — File + portal

When the start clears, the system marks them a funding client and asks for **documents**. Work waits on those docs.

Your job this day: find the file, set portal tasks, run **our** checklist. Not Alec’s Notion tracker. Not Slack as the book. Fundhub is the book.

**Quiz**

1. What holds a new funding file?  
2. Where do tasks live?  
3. May you start rounds before docs are accepted?

**Trainer key:** 1) Missing / unaccepted documents. 2) The Fundhub file / portal tasks. 3) No.

---

## Day 3 — Hands-off to specialists

If the file needs inquiry wipe or repair, you **hand** it. You do not become the specialist.

Clock: about **15** days is healthy. **30** is the hard stop. We do **not** use Alec’s 60 + 60 clocks.

Mail is **expedited** US mail. Never overnight. Never UPS or FedEx to a bureau.

**Quiz**

1. Who wipes inquiries?  
2. Who runs repair?  
3. What do you say about wait time?

**Trainer key:** 1) Inquiry seat (Specialist → Inquiries). 2) Repair seat (same login, Repair toggle, today). 3) Honest expedited wait. No score promise. No overnight.

---

## Day 4 — Sequence + first app

About **3–4** rounds. Three bureaus. Plan, then apply. Inquiry wipe can sit **between** rounds.

First card app: about 10 minutes at the desk, screen shared, on a **dummy** in camp. Do not submit a live application in training.

Do not invent a lender list or an APR. Rates are the lender’s.

**Quiz**

1. Why not spray every lender at once?  
2. When does inquiry work usually sit?  
3. May you promise which card they will get?

**Trainer key:** 1) Inquiries and file heat. We plan rounds. 2) Between rounds. 3) No.

---

## Day 5 — Client-start call (not a sales Present)

After pay, **you** run the first working call: file, portal, next steps. Inquiry or repair joins if the file needs them.

There is also a closer intake task in the repo to pull the credit host (CRS). If that clashes on a live file, ask a manager. Do not invent a third seat.

**Day 5 quiz (must miss zero)**

1. What is funding here?  
2. How do start fee and 10% fit?  
3. What is the file clock?  
4. When do you hand inquiry vs repair?  
5. What do you never promise on the start call?

**Trainer key:** 1) Help apply on a plan; lenders decide. 2) Start counts toward 10% if they get funded. 3) ~15 healthy / 30 hard. 4) Inquiries = hard looks / personal-info; repair = lates and bad marks. 5) Score up, sure fund, set dollar, overnight mail.

**Box 20:** Recorded mock client-start. Grade: portal shown, next step named, no sales close, no banned line.
