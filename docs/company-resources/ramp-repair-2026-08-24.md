**Never tell a client:**

- Their score will go up.
- A bad mark will come off.
- We will get them funded.

## Day 1 — See the marks

You look at lates and bad marks. The inquiry person looks at hard looks. Same report, different lens.

**Quiz**

1. What do you never promise about a mark?  
2. What do you never promise about a score?  
3. Are you the closer?

**Trainer key:** 1) That it will come off. 2) That it will go up. 3) No.

---

## Day 2 — The desk

Specialist → **Repair**. Empty copy you may see: “No repair files yet.” That is fine.

Personal-info errors may sit next to inquiry work. Do not steal the inquiry queue. Hand or switch only if that is the job for that hour.

**Quiz**

1. What does the side menu say?  
2. Which toggle is yours?  
3. What if the Repair list is empty?

**Trainer key:** 1) Specialist. 2) Repair. 3) Empty is real. Do not invent rows.

---

## Day 3 — Our path, not his overnight

His knockout pack is **reference**. We mail **expedited** US mail. Not overnight. Not UPS. Not FedEx to a bureau.

Build a plan for 2–3 items with **our** stages. If a stage is not on the dummy, write “not on this sample” — do not invent a bureau result.

**Quiz**

1. Overnight?  
2. May you use Alec’s 5-day knockout as our promise?  
3. What do you say about wait?

**Trainer key:** 1) No. Expedited US mail. 2) No. Reference only. 3) Honest wait. No score line.

---

## Day 4 — Hand back to funding

When the file was a funding+repair (or funding waiting on file work), the **funding advisor** owns rounds again. You write the hand-back. You do not start card apps.

**Quiz**

1. Who gets the file after repair work?  
2. Do you run the first card app?  
3. What goes in the hand-back note?

**Trainer key:** 1) Funding advisor (if this is a funding file). 2) No. 3) What you worked, what is still open, no outcome promise.

---

## Day 5 — Graduation

**Send letters** appears only when a letter body is ready. `POST` mail is a real click. Camp uses a dummy and **stops before live mail**.

**Day 5 quiz (must miss zero)**

1. Name three banned lines.  
2. How do we mail?  
3. When does Send letters show?  
4. What is the file clock?  
5. Who do you ping when a bureau answer needs a person?

**Trainer key:** 1) Score up / mark will come off / we will get you funded (or overnight). 2) Expedited US mail. 3) When a letter body is ready. 4) ~15 / 30. 5) Manager / the stuck path already on the Specialist desk (Specialist, owner, admin).

**Box 20:** Recorded mock. Grade: Repair toggle, file open, ready-to-send named, **no live send**, no banned line.
