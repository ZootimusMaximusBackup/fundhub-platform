Two numbers that decide everything

  • Break-even cost per sale is $635. Above that, the whole machine stops paying for itself. The front end alone breaks even at $280 — below that figure the $297 covers its own ad and every deposit behind it is free.
  • The cash comes back in seven days. Card money clears the processor in about a week, so the same bankroll gets spent roughly four times a month. You never need a month of ad budget sitting in the bank — you need seven days of it.
  • Repair buyers and funding buyers are different people. The 560-score buyer who can't be funded yet takes the $200 trial or the $1,000 done-for-you. Same ad, same $297, no extra cost to acquire them.
